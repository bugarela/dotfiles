#+title: Write On in Emacs
#+startup: showall

* Try the writing flow

The API has too many limitations. A smoother writing flow is needed on both computers.

The alternatives should be kept beside the prose. The document should remain readable when the editor is changed.

* Try the commands

1. Place point on “limitations” and press =SPC m w g=. Five alternatives are generated.
2. Press =j= or =k= in the panel to preview each version in the document. Press =RET= to keep the wording.
3. Return to the changed word and press =SPC m w c=. The saved versions are shown again.
4. Select a sentence and press =SPC m w g=. Alternatives are generated for the selection instead of a word.
5. Press =SPC m w G= to give a direction, such as “Make the wording more concrete”.
6. Press =a= in the panel to edit the highlighted option in an Org draft. Finish with Escape and then =SPC m w c=.
   Your draft is labelled “Mine”, and further AI alternatives are requested automatically. Cancel the draft with Escape and then =SPC m w k=.
7. Select unwanted prose and press =SPC m w s=. The prose is moved to a COMMENT Overflow subtree.
8. Press =SPC m w f= on an alternative to keep only the visible wording.

Press =q= or Escape in the panel to restore the starting wording. Saving is refused until the preview is kept or cancelled.

Normal editing and undo remain available. The chosen wording is included in Org exports. The saved alternatives and COMMENT subtrees are omitted.

This demo is opened as an unsaved buffer by =M-x write-on-demo=. Save the buffer with =C-x C-w= if the experiment should be kept.
